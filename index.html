RhythmIQ

Megacode Simulator

App developed by
Dr. Muhammed Alif
Consultant Anaesthesiologist & Intensivist
Founder, HealthionX Medical Solutions

RhythmIQ is a virtual bedside patient monitor for ACLS / BLS / PALS / Code Blue training. One device (laptop or projector) displays the patient monitor; another device (instructor's phone) acts as a wireless controller, syncing in real time over a shared room code.

Rhythms: 16 ECG rhythms across sinus, supraventricular, AV block, and ventricular/arrest categories, all generated mathematically in real time — including realistic PR-interval changes for 1st/2nd-degree AV blocks.

For training and educational use only. Not a medical device. Always follow institutional protocols and instructor guidance.
